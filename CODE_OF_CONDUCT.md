
# Terms & Conditions

## 1. Payment & Refund Policy

* Payment of $200 is required in full upon registration.
* No refunds will be provided after the course begins.
* If you cancel before the first session, a partial refund (minus processing fees) may be available.

## 2. Attendance & Participation

* Participants are expected to attend all sessions for the best experience.
* No recordings will be provided. 

## 3. Confidentiality & Privacy

* All discussions within the workshop should remain confidential.
* Personal data provided will only be used for course-related communications.

## 4. Code of Conduct

* Respectful communication is required at all times.
* Any disruptive behavior may result in removal from the workshop without a refund.

## 5. Technology & Access

* Participants must have a stable internet connection to attend via Zoom.
* The workshop link is for registered participants only and may not be shared.

## 6. Disclaimer

* This workshop is for educational purposes and does not replace professional therapy or counseling.
